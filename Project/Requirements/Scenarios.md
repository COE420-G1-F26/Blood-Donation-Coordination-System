## Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Restock Blood - Blood Bank Staff
Staff member John checks the inventory for available blood types and quantities. He notices that the blood bank has only 10 litres of AB+ blood. He checks the system to see if any donor drives are being organized. He sees that the blood for good foundation is organizing a donor drive from monday to thursday at the local clinic, which is affiliated to the blood bank. He notifies them of the shortage of AB+ blood and asks them to look for donors who are part of the blood group and collect blood from them.

### Scenario 2: Pre-position Stocks - Blood Bank Staff
Unfortunately, there was an earthquake in Helensville city. Civil services are rescuing victims under the rubble of destroyed buildings and transporting them to the local hospital. The town has 3 main hospitals and 5 clinics, all of which are affiliated with the blood bank. However, the blood bank is situated in the neighbouring Auckland city. St. John and McCarthy hospitals  are the biggest medical centers of the town and have large bed and blood storage capacity. Given the surge in victims, hospitals and clinics are contacting the blood bank for blood supplies. The blood bank fulfills urgent requests of all clinics and decides to preposition 7 liters of blood from all blood groups at the blood storage facilities in St.johns and McCarthy hospitals. Future requests of blood from other hospitals and clinics are fulfilled from stocks in these hospitals; reducing response times. The bank continuously monitors inventory of blood at the two hospitals, topping them up whenever they start to run low.

### Scenario 3: Organizing a Donation Drive - Drive Organizer
Sara is a volunteer at the Red Crescent. She wants to organize a blood donation drive at AUS for 3 days. She opens the system and creates a new drive by entering the title, location, dates, and a goal of 80 units. She also sets the target blood types to O+ and B-. During the drive, she checks the system to see how many donations have come in and which donors signed up. On the last day, she updates the drive page to remind donors to come before it closes, then closes the drive once it's done.
