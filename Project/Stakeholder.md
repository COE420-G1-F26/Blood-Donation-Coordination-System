Major stakeholders in our project:

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest/Expectations | Influence on the System | How the System Affects the Stakeholder
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Inventory Managers (Blood Bank Staff) | Manage the blood inventory and update blood quantities and types | They want the inventory to be accurate and easy to manage | Their requirements affect how the inventory part of the system is designed. (e.g.the usability, correctness, and reliability) | Makes it easier to track available blood and update the inventory (e.g.the usability, correctness, and reliability)
| Donation Drive Staff | Organize blood donation drives and manage donor registrations | They want an easy way to create and manage donation drives | Their needs affect the donation drive and registration features. (e.g. usability) | Helps them organize drives and keep track of registered donors.  (e.g. usability)
| Donors | Register for donation drives and donate blood | They want an easy way to find and register for donation drives | Their needs affect how simple and easy the registration process is (e.g. usability) | Makes registering for donation drives easier (e.g. usability)
| Hospital Staff | Search for available blood and request the required blood types and quantities | They want accurate information about available blood and an easy way to make requests | Their requirements affect the search, request, and reservation features. (e.g. usability, validity) | Helps them find and request blood more quickly. (e.g. usability, validity, performance)
| Doctors | Identify when blood is needed for patients and provide the required blood information to hospital staff. | They expect the required blood to be available when needed  | Their requirements can affect what information is included in blood requests and the speed which in blood can be requested) (e.g. correctness, and performance) | Helps them get the required blood through the hospital more efficiently. (e.g. performance)
| System Administrators | Manage the system and users | Want the system to be secure and work properly. (e.g. correctness, reliability, and security) | Help decide how user accounts and access work (e.g. usability amount) | Makes managing the system easier. (usability)
