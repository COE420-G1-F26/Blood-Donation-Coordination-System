## Project Objectives
The project aims to build a system that can manage the operations of a blood bank. It aims to centralize handling of blood inventories so hospitals across a geographic region can view available blood reserves and get access to blood quickly. It will also help blood banks organize donor drives.

## Target Users
### In Blood Banks
- Inventory Managers
- Staff who organize donor drives
- Staff who collect blood from donors

### In Hospitals
- Staff who request blood
- Doctors who identify need for blood

## In-Scope Features
Major features and functionalities of the project in scope:
- Receive blood from donors and add it to the system
- Multiple hospitals and blood banks will be able to see real-time inventories of blood.
- Users should be able to search for blood using blood type, etc.
- Blood bank should be able to organize donation drives
- Donors should be able to register for these drives and donate blood
- Hospitals should be able to request blood and book specific quantities.

## Out-of-Scope Features
Features or Functionalities not currently in scope of the project:
- Physical delivery of blood and its associated software systems
- Software for laboratory screening of blood to identify diseases, etc.
- Software systems associated with managing blood bank and hospital staff
- Extremely high rates of availability will not be targeted for the system in the initial stages, although scalability will be incorporated into the design.

## Major Deliverables
Major deliverables as part of the project:
- A detailed and complete list of requirements and use case diagrams for major functionalities.
- High level design of the system expressed in diagrams or documents
- Detailed design of the system expressed in UML diagrams
- Initial prototype to serve as proof of concept for the whole system.
